Section 1 — The 9-Stage Digital Color Workflow|The 9-stage digital color workflow is the structural framework for taking a ribbon SKU from brand color brief to bulk-production color approval: <em>Stage 1 — Digital Color Brief:</em> Brand provides digital color brief (Pantone TPX/TPG code, hex/RGB, Lab* reference, photo of physical reference, retail illuminant requirement). OEM logs brief in digital color management platform. <em>Stage 2 — Spectrophotometric Reference Reading:</em> OEM reads brand-supplied physical reference (lab-dip swatch, Pantone chip, sample) on X-Rite eXact or Konica Minolta CM-700d. Captures Lab* (L, a*, b*), ΔE vs Pantone, and specular / UV condition. <em>Stage 3 — Color Library Pull:</em> OEM checks the mill color library (Section 3) for closest match. If match is within ΔE <0.5, library pull is approved without re-dye. <em>Stage 4 — Virtual Color Render:</em> OEM generates virtual color render using ICC profile (Section 7) and substrate-color interaction data. Brand reviews virtual render against reference. <em>Stage 5 — Lab-Dip Production:</em> OEM produces lab-dip on the actual production substrate. Lab-dip volume: 5-50 m. <em>Stage 6 — Lab-Dip Spectrophotometric Reading:</em> Lab-dip read on spectrophotometer. ΔE vs brand reference calculated. If ΔE >2.0, lab-dip fails. If ΔE 1.0-2.0, brand reviews. If ΔE ≤1.0, lab-dip approved. <em>Stage 7 — Lab-Dip Brand Approval:</em> Brand reviews lab-dip (physical + digital). Approval documented in PantoneLive. <em>Stage 8 — Bulk Production Color Check:</em> Bulk production begins. In-line color check at 1st 50 m, 25% mark, 50% mark, 75% mark, 100% mark. <em>Stage 9 — Final Color Approval:</em> Final color approval based on in-line readings, bulk sample, and brand color manager review. The 9-stage workflow compresses color approval from 14-21 days to 5-7 days and delivers 96% first-shot color match.
