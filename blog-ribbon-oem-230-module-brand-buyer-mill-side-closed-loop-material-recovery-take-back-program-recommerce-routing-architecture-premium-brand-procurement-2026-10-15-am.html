3. The 5-Pillar Closed-Loop-Routing Governance Framework

The 5-pillar closed-loop-routing governance framework sequences (1) Take-Back-Policy-Pillar — explicit take-back-policy with mass-balance accounting per ISO 14021; (2) Take-Back-Logistics-Pillar — consumer-collection-points via parcel networks (DHL/FDX return, UPS Returns), retailer-take-back-counters via boutique + department-store + gift-shop networks, brand-bulk-recovery bins via DC-routing; (3) Take-Back-Pricing-Pillar — consumer-side deposit-incentive (USD 0.50-2 per 100m reel), retailer-side rebate (1-4% of next PO), brand-side bulk-recovery negotiated-rate; (4) Take-Back-Logistics-Pillar — parcel-network routing, retailer-counter routing, brand-bulk DC routing; (5) Take-Back-Evidence-Pillar — weekly-volume log, recovery-mill audit-trail, closed-loop-claim-substantiation ledger per California-Prop-65 + EU-Green-Claims-Directive + UK-Green-Claims-Code.

4. The 3-Tier Closed-Loop-Class Assignment Framework

The 3-tier closed-loop-class assignment framework classifies ribbon trim into recovery-tiers based on material composition, recycling-stream eligibility, and brand-buyer program-maturity: Tier-1-Consumer-Recommerce for PET-only recycled-content monomaterial ribbon (95-98% recovery-yield); Tier-2-Retailer-Take-Back for PET-blend ribbon with cellulosic-blend or foil-thread (78-88% recovery-yield); Tier-3-Brand-Bulk-Recovery for mixed-fiber + foil + latex-blend ribbon requiring mill-side mechanical-recycling (54-68% recovery-yield). Closed-loop-class assignment drives take-back-routing-protocol, take-back-pricing-policy, and take-back-evidence-aggregation depth.
