Who We Are

Smith Ribbon is the direct-factory export brand of Xiamen Smith Ribbon & Bow Co., Ltd. (厦门思蜜丝织带饰品有限公司). We specialize in OEM/ODM ribbon and decorative bow manufacturing for the global gift packaging, festive decoration, hair accessory, and apparel trim industries.

Our 15,000㎡ vertically integrated factory in Xiamen controls every step — from yarn sourcing and weaving, through dyeing, printing, and bow-tying, to final QC and export packaging. With 50+ production lines and 100,000m daily capacity, we deliver consistent quality at scale.

Why Brands Choose Smith Ribbon

  • True direct factory. No middlemen, no markups. You talk to the people who make your ribbon.
  • Flexible MOQ. 500m for stock styles, 1,000m for custom. Perfect for both indie brands and large retailers.
  • Retail-grade certifications. OEKO-TEX, BSCI, ISO 9001, SEDEX, SMETA — full documentation included.
  • Speed & reliability. 7-day sampling, 15-25 day production for most orders. 24h reply on WhatsApp.
  • Sustainability-ready. RPET recycled polyester, bamboo fiber, FSC paper packaging available.
