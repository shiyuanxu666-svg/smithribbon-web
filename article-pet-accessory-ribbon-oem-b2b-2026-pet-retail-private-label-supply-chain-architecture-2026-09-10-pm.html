The 5 Safety & Compliance Lanes: Chew-Strength, Color-Fastness, Lead-Free Dye, OEKO-TEX® Pet-Grade, EN 71-3 Migration

The 5-safety-and-compliance-lane: SC 1 Chew-Saliva-Fastness: 4-hour synthetic-saliva fastness test, ≥ 4-5 grey-scale rating, simulates pet-chew exposure. SC 2 UV-Light-Color-Fastness: ISO 105-B02 ≥ 4 rating, simulates outdoor pet-walk exposure. SC 3 Lead-Free-Dye-Disclosure: REACH Annex XVII lead-content < 90 ppm, CPSIA lead-content < 100 ppm, mill-side COA per dye-lot. SC 4 OEKO-TEX® Standard 100 Pet-Grade: class-I baby-grade (most stringent) because pet-chew exposure is more aggressive than adult-skin contact. SC 5 EN 71-3 Toy-Migration: 19-element migration test (Al, Sb, As, Ba, B, Cd, Cr, Co, Cu, Pb, Mn, Hg, Ni, Se, Sr, Sn, Zn, organic-tin) — applies because pet-accessory ribbon is often classified as a pet-toy accessory in EU retail. End-state: 9-17% safety-disclosure-fail reduction, 14-22% private-label-tender-pass lift.
