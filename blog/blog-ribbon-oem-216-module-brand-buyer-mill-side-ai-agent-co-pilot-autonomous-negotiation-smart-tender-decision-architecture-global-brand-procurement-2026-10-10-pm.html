3. The 5-Pillar Guardrail (Human-in-Loop, Explainability, Audit, Jurisdiction, Blast-Radius)

The 5-pillar guardrail protects brand-buyer against AI-hallucination, autonomous-overreach, IP-leak, unfair-trade-practice, and labor-rights-violation. It satisfies EU-AI-Act Article-13 / 14 / 9 transparency, human-oversight, and risk-management requirements, US-EO-14110 safety-order requirements, China-GenAI-Rules training-data-transparency, Singapore-MGF, and California-BOT-Disclosure-Act:

  • Pillar-1 Human-in-Loop: every agent-tool-call above USD 1,000 / 1,000-meter PO-equivalent triggers human-approval; below that floor the agent can auto-commit.
  • Pillar-2 Explainability-Trace: every recommendation emits a step-by-step rationale (inputs / weight / model-version / guardrail-rule / override-history) as JSON / PDF / web-renderable.
  • Pillar-3 Model-Versioning-and-Audit-Log: every model-version is pinned in a registry; every commit emits an audit-log entry; brand-buyer can replay 12-24 months of decisions.
  • Pillar-4 Jurisdiction-Law-Stack: EU-AI-Act / US-EO-14110 / China-GenAI-Rules / Singapore-MGF / UK-AI-Bill / California-BOT-Disclosure-Act / Brazil-PLG-227/2024 calibrated per brand-buyer-region.
  • Pillar-5 Sandbox-and-Blast-Radius: every tool-call is sandboxed; max-commit-per-cycle capped at USD 8,000-22,000; auto-revoke-on-anomaly.
