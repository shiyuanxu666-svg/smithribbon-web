28-Module Architecture Framework: Six Layers, 28 Modules, 100% Brand-Auditable

The 28-module framework organizes talent and knowledge-management into six logical layers: (1) Critical-Role Mapping, Recruitment & Onboarding, (2) Competency, Certification & Apprenticeship, (3) Performance, OKR & Talent-Review, (4) Learning, Coaching & Leadership Pipeline, (5) Engagement, Retention, DEI & Wellbeing, and (6) Knowledge-Base, Lessons-Learned & HR-IT Integration. Each layer carries between 3 and 9 modules, and every module has a defined owner (HR director, line manager, functional head, OEM general manager, OEKO-TEX compliance officer, training lead, knowledge-management lead), a defined input (critical-role list, job description, candidate profile, competency map, OKR cascade, training catalog, lesson-learned entry), a defined output (hire, onboarding-completion, certification, OKR score, training-completion, retention-risk flag, knowledge-base article, community-of-practice meeting), and a defined consumer (brand procurement, brand compliance auditor, brand quality team, OEM line manager, OEM HR). The framework is intentionally scalable: a 60-employee ribbon OEM can run a 28-module lite version, and a 600-employee multi-plant ribbon OEM can run the full 28-module enterprise version with multiple plants and a dedicated L&D function.

6-Critical-Role-Map, 7-Recruitment-Pipeline & 8-Onboarding

Modules 1 through 21 govern the foundational people layer. The 6 critical-role modules cover critical-role definition (a role whose vacancy would halt a key account within 30 days), critical-role identification (typically 8-14 critical roles per OEM), critical-role incumbent profile, critical-role-successor flag, critical-role-pay-band, and critical-role-recruitment SLA. The 7 recruitment-pipeline modules cover job-description library, recruitment-channel mix (campus, online, referral, headhunter, internal mobility, walk-in, returned overseas talent), candidate-screening rubrics, candidate-assessment (technical test, behavior-based interview, work-sample, reference-check, background-check, language test, culture-fit), offer-and-closing, offer-letter-to-joining-time tracking, and 90-day new-hire check-in. The 8 onboarding modules cover pre-joining documentation, day-1 orientation, week-1 floor-walk, week-2 mentor-pairing, month-1 first-deliverable, month-2 competency-baseline, month-3 first-mini-project, and 6-month full-productivity gate. The 21 modules in this layer turn every new hire from a 6-12 month unknown into a 3-6 month productive contributor.

9-Cross-Functional-Rotation, 7-Competency-Matrix & 6-Cert-Trade-Test

Modules 22 through 43 govern the competency and skill-building layer. The 9 cross-functional-rotation modules cover weaving-to-dyeing rotation, dyeing-to-printing rotation, printing-to-bow-assembly rotation, bow-assembly-to-QC rotation, QC-to-warehouse rotation, warehouse-to-merchandising rotation, merchandising-to-account-management rotation, account-management-to-planning rotation, and planning-to-IE rotation. The 7 competency-matrix modules cover role-by-competency map, proficiency level (1-5 scale), current-vs-target proficiency gap, training-need analysis, certification-renewal cadence, competency-archive, and competency-benchmark vs. industry. The 6 cert-trade-test modules cover loom-operator certification, dye-master certification, color-matching certification, print-master certification, bow-assembly certification, and quality-engineer certification. The 22 modules in this layer are what transform a trade-skill workforce into a certified, multi-skill, rotation-ready workforce that can flex with seasonal demand and sudden capacity shifts.

8-Apprentice-Master, 5-Mentorship & 6-Succession

Modules 44 through 62 govern the long-term talent layer. The 8 apprentice-master modules cover apprentice intake, master-craftsman roster, master-craftsman training-of-trainer cert, apprentice-master pairing, apprentice skill-checkpoint (typically every 90 days), apprentice portfolio build, apprentice graduation, and master-craftsman retention bonus. The 5 mentorship modules cover mentor-mentee pairing, mentor-cadre selection, mentor-cadence (typically bi-weekly), mentor-1-on-1-format, and mentor-MBO (mentor business objective). The 6 succession modules cover succession-candidate identification, succession-candidate-readiness (1-2 years ready, 3-5 years ready), succession-acceleration plan, succession-risk score, succession-transition playbook, and succession-communication to stakeholders. The 19 modules in this layer are what keep a 20-year-old ribbon OEM from losing its craft knowledge when a master weaver retires.
