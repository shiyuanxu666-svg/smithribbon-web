27-Module Architecture Framework: Six Layers, 27 Modules, 100% Brand-Approved

The 27-module framework organizes brand-equity and trade-marketing into six logical layers: (1) In-Store Visual Merchandising & Planogram Compliance, (2) Co-Marketing, Influencer & Earned-Media, (3) Activations, Loyalty & Sampling, (4) Trade Promotion, Channel Margin & Retail Media, (5) Display, Pack-Size & Channel Architecture, and (6) Annual Co-Investment, KPI Dashboard & Governance. Each layer carries between 3 and 8 modules, and each module has a defined owner (brand trade-marketing director, brand category buyer, OEM account manager, OEM merchandising lead, OEM digital-content lead, retailer buyer, licensor marketing lead, agency partner, 3PL merchandiser), a defined input (retail banner planogram, seasonal floor-set calendar, co-marketing budget, trade-promotion calendar, co-investment fund, KPI dashboard), a defined output (planogram-compliant shelf, seasonal floor-set, co-branded launch, pop-up activation, trade-promotion ROI report, annual co-investment scorecard), and a defined consumer (retail merchant, end-consumer, licensor, brand marketing team, brand finance). The framework is intentionally channel-agnostic: big-box retail, specialty retail, off-price retail, e-commerce, marketplace, and DTC can all plug in using the same 27 modules.
