A brand-buyer running on disconnected mill-side data is structurally exposed to all three vectors.

3. The 5-Domain Ontology

The 5-domain ontology defines a canonical data-model across the mill-network:

  • Substrate-Domain: yarn-batch, fiber-spec, OEKO-TEX-supplier, FSC-certificate, GRS-certificate (22-30 entities).
  • Production-Domain: machine-set, dye-formula-version, dyelot-traceability, AQL-defect-rate, OEE-yield (30-50 entities).
  • Quality-Domain: color-matching-dE, light-fastness, crock-fastness, wash-fastness, shrinkage, hand-feel (40-60 entities).
  • Logistics-Domain: freight-booking, port-of-dispatch, ETA, container-load, last-mile-status (25-35 entities).
  • Commercial-Domain: PO, ASN, invoice, payment-term, FX-hedge, customs-clearance (30-50 entities).

7. The 4-Tier Governance Architecture

The 4-tier governance architecture sequences data-quality / data-security / data-lineage / data-privacy tiers:

  • Tier 1 (Data-Quality): 6-stage quality-checks (schema-validation, completeness, accuracy, timeliness, consistency, uniqueness) on every silver-record write.
  • Tier 2 (Data-Security): AES-256 encryption at-rest, TLS-1.3 in-transit, ISO-27001 access-control, SOC-2-audit-trail.
  • Tier 3 (Data-Lineage): end-to-end lineage from raw-event source (mill-MES, brand-ERP, freight-booking) to gold-curated-KPI; supports 100% audit-trail.
  • Tier 4 (Data-Privacy): GDPR / CCPA / PIPL-compliance, brand-buyer-PII-tokenization, 30-day right-to-erasure, 7-year audit-log retention.
