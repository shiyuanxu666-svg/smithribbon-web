3.1 Layer 1 — 11-Eco-Modulated-Cadre

The eco-modulation cadre converts the EU ESPR (Ecodesign for Sustainable Products Regulation) framework into ribbon-specific design rules. For each SKU, the cadre outputs 9 eco-modulation levers: recycled-content target, mono-material structure, dye-stuff toxicity, finishing chemistry VOC, water-reclaim, energy-source mix, packaging-mass, end-of-life recyclability, and durability / wash-fastness. The cadre's decision matrix is then piped directly to the procurement specification so a brand-buyer RFQ can lock the 9 levers at RFQ stage rather than discover them at sample-review.

For the 86+ brand partners, this module reduces the time-to-eco-modulation-spec from 6-9 weeks to 1-2 weeks, and eliminates 31-46% of the eco-modulation re-work that delays ESPR-aligned launches.

3.2 Layer 2 — 12-ACCU-Engine

The ACCU (Assigned Carbon Credit Unit) engine reconciles the carbon-credit ledger between mill, upstream feedstock supplier and downstream brand owner. Because Xiamen mill draws on a mix of GRS-claimed rPET flake, ISCC-certified bio-attributed yarn, and conventional virgin polyester, the ACCU engine reconciles the upstream credit retirement with the mill's physical-flow claim and the brand's downstream disclosure. The engine outputs a single ACCU balance per SKU and aligns retirement certificates with the corresponding purchase order, heat number and PO lot.

3.3 Layer 3 — 10-PCF-Pipeline

The PCF (Product Carbon Footprint) pipeline builds cradle-to-grave emissions per SKU. For a typical 12 mm printed satin ribbon, the pipeline integrates 5 main stages: yarn extrusion → weaving → dyeing & finishing → printing / hot-stamp → cut & pack. Each stage receives an emission factor from the mill's primary-data pool, with the printed / hot-stamp stage carrying the most variability (3-6× the base satin per-meter value) depending on ink chemistry and metallization. The pipeline outputs both single-issue PCF (cradle-to-gate) and a cradle-to-customer-grave PCF for D2C brands.

3.4 Layer 4 — 9-PCF-API-Stack

The PCF-API stack is the integration layer that lets a brand's internal sustainability platform ( Watershed, Persefoni, Salesforce Net Zero Cloud, Microsoft Sustainability Manager ) pull PCF, ACCU and eco-modulation data on demand. The stack exposes 6 API endpoints: /pcf, /accu, /ecomod, /recycledcontent, /cert, /sku. Each endpoint is rate-limited to 60 calls/min, OAuth2-protected, and logs every retrieval for assurance-trail purposes. The stack eliminates the spreadsheet email chain that historically cost brands 11-18 hours per PCF refresh.

3.5 Layer 5 — 8-Eco-Archive & 7-PCF-Dashboard

The archive module records 36 months of monthly eco-modulation, ACCU retirement and PCF snapshots with primary-data evidence. The dashboard surfaces the same data as a live KPI cockpit, with brand-buyer team-level read access and a sister write-access for sustainability controllers to approve quarterly re-attestations. A red-amber-green signal flag triggers a 14-day re-attestation window whenever any eco-modulation lever drifts more than 5% from baseline.

3.6 Layer 6 — 9-Eco-IP, 6-Eco-Cost & 10-Eco-Continuous-Improvement

The IP module protects brand-specific eco-modulation methodologies behind scoped access tokens and a brand-tagging convention. The cost module aligns eco-modulation with landed-cost math so a brand sees that a 5.4% ribbon cost increase from a recycled-content lift is offset by a 7.6% shipping-cost decrease once a 14-meter-kg shipment consolidates three SKUs. The CI module runs quarterly eco-modulation retrofits: bio-attributed yarn share, dye-stuff VOC reduction, finishing chemistry, and 6-8% annual PCF reduction targets.
