6. How Smith Ribbon Operationalises the 102-Module Program

Smith Ribbon runs the 102-module brand-buyer mill-side biogenic-CO2 land-use-change bio-attributed & bio-sourced allocation architecture program as a quarterly cadence. Step 1 Biogenic-CO2-Inventory: the team computes biogenic-CO2 emission per SKU per quarter using the GHG-Protocol biogenic-CO2 method. Step 2 Land-Use-Change-Map: the team maps land-use change per bio-sourced input, applies a 1% cut-off rule, and discloses the land-use-change hectare. Step 3 Bio-Attributed-Input-Qualification: the team qualifies every bio-attributed input, applies mass-balance accounting, and exposes the bio-attributed mass-balance certificate. Step 4 Bio-Sourced-Supply-Reservation: the team pre-books bio-sourced supply per quarter, traces every batch, and exposes the bio-sourced supply certificate. Step 5 Bio-Allocation-Choice: the team selects mass-allocation, economic-allocation, or system-expansion per ISO-14044, documents the choice, and recalibrates every quarter. Step 6 Bio-Allocation-Disclosure: the team publishes the bio-allocation choice, the bio-allocation sensitivity, the bio-allocation audit trail, and the bio-allocation recalc log. Step 7 Bio-Assurance: the team commissions a third-party limited-assurance or reasonable-assurance review per ISO-14064-3 and exposes the assurance opinion. Step 8 Bio-Disclosure & Quarterly Recalc: the team maps every bio-output to CSRD-ESRS-E1, CSRD-ESRS-E5, EU-CBAM, CDP-Climate, and SBTi, recalculates the bio-inventory every quarter, and publishes the new disclosure.
