26-Module Architecture Framework: Six Layers, 26 Modules, 100% Brand-Approved

The 26-module framework organizes aftermarket service and reverse-logistics into six logical layers: (1) Customer-Issue Triage & Return Authorization, (2) Recovery, Refurbish & Relist, (3) Field-Failure Root-Cause & Warranty, (4) Consumer-Complaint, Social-Listening & Channel-Return, (5) Refurbished-Channel, Sustainability & Recycle Stream, and (6) Recall, Crisis-Communications & Continuous-Improvement. Each layer carries between 3 and 8 modules, and each module has a defined owner (brand customer-service lead, brand quality manager, OEM account manager, OEM RMA coordinator, OEM production planner, OEM CAPA engineer, 3PL reverse-logistics partner, licensor compliance officer), a defined input (consumer complaint, retail merchant claim, RMA request, return shipment, lab sample, recall notice, social-listening alert), a defined output (triage tag, RMA number, rework lot, refurbish-and-relist lot, credit note, warranty decision, recall execution plan, CAPA action plan), and a defined consumer (brand finance, brand customer-service, retail merchant, licensor, end-consumer, regulatory authority). The framework is intentionally channel-agnostic: DTC e-commerce, big-box retail, specialty retail, marketplace channels, and licensed-product programs can all plug in using the same 26 modules.

6-Customer-Issue-Triage, 7-Return-Authorization & 8-Rework-Recovery

Modules 1 through 21 govern the customer-issue and return layer. The 6 customer-issue-triage modules cover intake channel (DTC email, DTC chat, retail call-center, marketplace buyer-message, social-media DM, licensor notice), triage category (color-shift, width-tolerance miss, handfeel change, edge-finish defect, print-registration miss, label-error), triage severity (cosmetic, functional, safety, regulatory, brand-damaging), triage SLA (24 hours for safety and regulatory, 72 hours for cosmetic and functional), triage owner (brand customer-service vs. OEM account manager), and triage escalation path. The 7 return-authorization modules cover RMA-number generation, RMA-window policy (typically 30-90 days for retail, 30 days for DTC, custom for licensed), RMA-reason-code taxonomy, RMA-freight-responsibility (DDP vs. DDU, prepaid-label vs. carrier-billed), RMA-receipt-and-inspection, RMA-restock-fee policy, and RMA-credit-memo issuance. The 8 rework-recovery modules cover rework-triage (sortable vs. non-sortable, re-dyeable vs. non-redyeable), rework-by-color-shift (re-dye vs. blend-out), rework-by-handfeel (re-softener, re-starch), rework-by-width (re-slitting, re-welding), rework-by-edge (re-heat-cut, re-ultrasonic-cut, re-wire-insertion), rework-by-print (re-print, re-stamp, re-foil), rework-by-finishing (re-flame-retardant, re-water-repellent, re-anti-stat), and rework-QA-and-relist. The 21 modules in this layer are what turn a returned SKU into either a re-shippable asset, a credit-memo, or a controlled disposal.

9-Refurbish-Relist, 7-Field-Failure-Root-Cause & 6-Customer-Compensation

Modules 22 through 43 govern the recovery and root-cause layer. The 9 refurbish-and-relist modules cover refurbish-by-grade (Grade-A: 100% re-ship, Grade-B: 70-90% margin channels, Grade-C: donation / recycle), refurbish-by-channel (DTC re-ship, marketplace re-ship, retail return-to-vendor, B-stock clearance, off-price channel, export channel), refurbish-by-packaging (re-pack, re-label, re-tag, re-sticker), refurbish-by-doc (re-issue certificate, re-issue test-report, re-issue EPR-number), refurbish-by-traceability (re-tag lot-number, re-tag PO-number, re-tag ship-date), refurbish-by-shelf-life (re-date code, re-expiration, re-pull-window), refurbish-by-compliance (re-CPSIA, re-Prop-65, re-GB-4806, re-Oeko-Tex), refurbish-by-photo (re-shoot for A+ content, re-shoot for marketplace listing, re-shoot for retail planogram), and refurbish-by-pricing (Grade-A at 100% price, Grade-B at 60-80% price, Grade-C at 20-40% price). The 7 field-failure-root-cause modules cover failure-intake, failure-categorization, failure-investigation (5-Why, Ishikawa, fault-tree), failure-lab-analysis (microscopy, FTIR, color-spectro, dyestuff-HPLC), failure-trace-back (lot-number, PO-number, production-shift, raw-material-lot), failure-statistics (Pareto, control-chart, capability-index), and failure-finding (root-cause statement). The 6 customer-compensation modules cover refund, replacement, credit-memo, future-order-discount, brand-goodwill-credit, and licensor-royalty-adjustment. The 22 modules in this layer are what keep the brand-customer relationship intact after a failure.

5-Credit-Note-Management, 8-Warranty-Claim & 6-Spare-Parts-Stock

Modules 44 through 62 govern the credit and warranty layer. The 5 credit-note-management modules cover credit-note-issuance trigger, credit-note-amount calculation (replacement-cost, freight-cost, handling-cost, margin-recovery), credit-note-accounting treatment, credit-note-aging KPI, and credit-note-audit (typically annual, by brand finance and OEM finance). The 8 warranty-claim modules cover warranty-period definition (typically 30-90 days for retail, 30 days for DTC, 12 months for licensed, custom for brand-enterprise), warranty-coverage scope (manufacturing-defect only vs. includes wear-and-tear, includes color-fade, includes packaging-defect), warranty-claim-intake, warranty-claim-investigation, warranty-claim-decision (covered vs. not-covered), warranty-claim-fulfillment (refund vs. replacement vs. repair), warranty-claim-appeal path, and warranty-claim-prevention (root-cause looped back into CAPA). The 6 spare-parts-stock modules cover spare-parts SKUs (typically a small subset: bows with replaceable clips, headbands with replaceable elastic, gift-wrap with replaceable ribbon-cards), spare-parts-MOQ, spare-parts-pricing, spare-parts-warehouse, spare-parts-3PL-routing, and spare-parts-discontinuation-policy. The 19 modules in this layer are what keep the warranty cost predictable and bankable.

7-Consumer-Complaint, 5-Social-Listening & 8-Trade-Channel-Return

Modules 63 through 82 govern the consumer-complaint and trade-channel layer. The 7 consumer-complaint modules cover complaint-intake (DTC email, DTC chat, marketplace buyer-message, social-media DM, retail call-center, licensor notice, regulatory-authority letter), complaint-categorization (product-quality, packaging, label, shipping-damage, misshipment, allergen, child-safety), complaint-SLA (24 hours for safety and allergen, 72 hours for quality and packaging, 5 days for shipping), complaint-investigation, complaint-resolution, complaint-customer-communications, and complaint-prevention. The 5 social-listening modules cover platform-listening (Instagram, TikTok, Twitter/X, YouTube, Reddit, Trustpilot, Yelp, Google Reviews), brand-mention-monitor, sentiment-analysis, escalation-trigger, and response-coordination. The 8 trade-channel-return modules cover big-box retail RTV (return-to-vendor), specialty retail RTV, mass-market RTV, dollar-store RTV, club-store RTV, off-price RTV, export-channel RTV, and cross-border RTV. The 20 modules in this layer are what catch the issues before they become recall or regulatory events.

4-Cross-Border-RMA, 6-Refurbished-Channel, 7-Sustainability-Recovery, 5-Donation-Charity & 4-Recycle-Stream

Modules 83 through 106 govern the refurbished and sustainability layer. The 4 cross-border-RMA modules cover cross-border-RMA-intake, cross-border-freight-routing, cross-border-customs-clearance, and cross-border-credit-memo. The 6 refurbished-channel modules cover refurbished-DTC-channel, refurbished-marketplace-channel (Amazon-Outlet, eBay-Refurbished, Wayfair-Open-Box, TikTok-Shop-Refurbished), refurbished-B2B-channel, refurbished-off-price-channel (TJ Maxx, Marshalls, Ross, Burlington), refurbished-export-channel, and refurbished-clearance-channel. The 7 sustainability-recovery modules cover sustainability-recovery-policy, sustainability-recovery-volume-KPI, sustainability-recovery-tier (reuse > refurbish > recycle > energy-recovery > disposal), sustainability-recovery-vendor (textile-recycler, packaging-recycler, fiber-recycler, polyester-recycler, RPET-recycler), sustainability-recovery-certificate (GRS, RCS, SCS-Recycled-Content, Oeko-Tex-Recycled), sustainability-recovery-audit, and sustainability-recovery-reporting (GRI, SASB, CSRD, ESRS). The 26 modules in this layer are what turn reverse-logistics from a cost-center into a sustainability and margin contributor.

5-IT-System, 4-Recall-Trigger, 6-Recall-Execution & 4-Crisis-Communications

Modules 107 through 125 govern the IT and recall layer. The 5 IT-system modules cover the RMA-tracking system, the refurbish-tracking system, the credit-note-tracking system, the consumer-complaint-tracking system, and the field-failure-CAPA-tracking system. The 4 recall-trigger modules cover recall-trigger-criteria (CPSIA, REACH, Prop-65, GB-4806, Oeko-Tex, FDA, licensor-mandate, brand-voluntary), recall-trigger-decision-matrix, recall-trigger-decision-owner (brand compliance vs. OEM compliance vs. licensor compliance), and recall-trigger-SLA. The 4 crisis-communications modules cover crisis-comms-team, crisis-comms-message-template, crisis-comms-channel-mix, and crisis-comms-regulatory-coordination. 5-Post-Recall-CAPA & 3-Continuous-Improvement Modules

Modules 126 through 133 close the loop. The 5 post-recall-CAPA modules cover CAPA-1 corrective-action (immediate containment), CAPA-2 root-cause-validation, CAPA-3 preventive-action (design, process, supplier, training, labeling), CAPA-4 effectiveness-check, and CAPA-5 closure-and-archive. Together, the 8 modules in this layer ensure that the 26-module aftermarket service and reverse-logistics lifecycle architecture is a learning system, not a static system.
