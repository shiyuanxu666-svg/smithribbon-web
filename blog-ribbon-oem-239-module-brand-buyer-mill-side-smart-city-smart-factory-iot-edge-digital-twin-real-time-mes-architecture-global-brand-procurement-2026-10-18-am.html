7.2 What is the difference between OPC UA, MQTT, and ISA-95 in the 239-module stack?

OPC UA is the data-exchange protocol between mill stations and the MES layer. MQTT is the lightweight publish-subscribe used for low-bandwidth IoT-edge sensors. ISA-95 / B2MML is the integration standard between MES and ERP / supply-chain systems. The 239-module architecture uses OPC UA at the gateway, MQTT at the edge sensor layer, and ISA-95 / B2MML at the MES-to-ERP boundary.

7.3 How does 239-module architecture interact with ISO 23247 digital-twin standard?

ISO 23247 defines a four-layer digital-twin reference architecture (object, data, service, integration). The 239-module IoT-edge MES maps cleanly onto ISO 23247: stages 1-2 of the 6-stage pipeline are the object layer, stages 3-4 are the data layer, stage 5 is the service layer, and stage 6 is the integration layer.
