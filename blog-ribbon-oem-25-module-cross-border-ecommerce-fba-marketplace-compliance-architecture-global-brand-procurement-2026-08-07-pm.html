25-Module Architecture Framework: Six Layers, 25 Modules, 100% Marketplace-Approved

H2-2|The 25-module framework organizes cross-border ribbon e-commerce into six logical layers: (1) Marketplace Onboarding & Listing Compliance, (2) Packaging, Labeling & Barcode Compliance, (3) Product Safety, Hazmat & EPR Compliance, (4) FBA Prep, 3PL Routing & Reverse Logistics, (5) Promotion, Margin & Account Health, and (6) Tax, Currency, Dispute & IT-Integration. Each layer has between 3 and 8 modules, and each module has a defined owner (marketplace-seller, brand compliance officer, OEM account manager, OEM pre-production planner, 3PL partner, customs broker), a defined input (SKU master, marketplace template, hazmat declaration, FNSKU, GS1 barcode, EPR registration), a defined output (live listing, prepped carton, FBA inbound, customs-cleared shipment, customer review), and a defined consumer (marketplace algorithm, marketplace seller, brand finance, customs authority, end-customer). The framework is intentionally marketplace-agnostic: Amazon FBA, TikTok Shop, Tmall Global, Shopee, Lazada, Etsy, eBay, Wayfair, Faire, and dozens of regional marketplaces can all be onboarded using the same 25 modules, with marketplace-specific clauses configured per program.

6-Marketplace Onboarding, 7-Listing-Compliance Workflow & 8-Packaging-Compliance Stack

H2-3|Modules 1 through 21 govern the marketplace-onboarding layer. The 6 marketplace-onboarding modules cover marketplace identity, marketplace territory, marketplace category coverage (ribbon, bow, gift-wrap, packaging-supply, party-supply, stationery, craft, beauty-accessory, candle-accessory), marketplace account type (1P, 3P, Pro, Business, Flagship), marketplace sub-account and delegated admin, and marketplace brand-registry enrollment. The 7 listing-compliance-workflow modules cover title template, bullet-point template, key-attribute template, A+ content / rich-description, search-term / keyword, main-image spec, and secondary-image spec. The 8 packaging-compliance-stack modules cover polybag warning (suffocation warning on polybag > 5mil thick for child-safety), FBA carton label (FNSKU, suffocation warning if applicable, country-of-origin, multi-language warning), FBA prep requirements (polybag, bubble-wrap, dunnage, set-pack, sticker, suffocation warning, fragile sticker), TikTok-Shop packaging, Tmall-Global packaging, Shopee-Lazada packaging, Etsy-eBay packaging, and Wayfair-Faire packaging. The 21 modules in this layer are what make a SKU marketplace-ready.

9-Labeling-and-Barcode Stack, 7-Hazmat-and-Product-Safety Stack & 6-EPR-Extended-Producer-Responsibility

H2-4|Modules 22 through 43 govern the labeling, hazmat, and EPR layer. The 9 labeling-and-barcode-stack modules cover GS1 GTIN-13 / GTIN-14 barcode (with prefix allocation by territory), GS1 company prefix, UPC-A, EAN-13, FNSKU, ASIN, country-of-origin label (e.g., "Made in China"), fiber-content label (e.g., "100% Polyester"), care label, hangtag, polybag sticker, master-carton label, and pallet label. The 7 hazmat-and-product-safety-stack modules cover lithium-battery-free declaration (most ribbon SKUs are non-hazmat, but a sub-tier battery or magnetic component can trigger hazmat), choking-hazard warning (small parts, beads, charms), Prop-65 (California), CPSIA (US children-product), REACH SVHC declaration (EU), GB 4806 food-contact (China), and JP Food-Sanitation (Japan). The 6 EPR-and-extended-producer-responsibility modules cover France EPR (packaging, textile, paper), Germany EPR (LUCID, Verpackungsgesetz, EAR), Spain EPR (REI), UK EPR (packaging waste, plastic tax), EU PPWR (packaging and packaging waste regulation 2025/40), and US state EPR (CA SB-54, OR, ME, WA, CO, MN). The 22 modules in this layer are what keep a SKU from being delisted, recalled, or fined by a marketplace.

8-Amazon-FBA-Prep Workflow, 7-TikTok-Shop-Listing Workflow & 6-Tmall-Global-Flagship Workflow

H2-5|Modules 44 through 64 govern the major-marketplace workflow layer. The 8 Amazon-FBA-prep modules cover FBA inbound shipment creation, FNSKU labeling, polybag warning, set-pack, bubble-wrap, dunnage, master-carton weight & dimension, and FBA box-content statement. The 7 TikTok-Shop-listing modules cover TikTok-Shop seller registration, TikTok-Shop category permission, TikTok-Shop listing-attribute (size, color, material, package-included, age-group, hazard-type), TikTok-Shop pre-shipment photo, TikTok-Shop creator-affiliate onboarding, TikTok-Shop LIVE-streaming launch-kit, and TikTok-Shop 7-day return policy. The 6 Tmall-Global-flagship modules cover Tmall-Global store qualification (brand owner or authorized distributor), Tmall-Global category permission, Tmall-Global listing (Chinese-language, RMB-pricing, China-bonded-warehouse or direct-ship from origin), Tmall-Global brand-zone design, Tmall-Global 11.11 / 6.18 / CNY campaign calendar, and Tmall-Global cross-border return flow. The 21 modules in this layer are the high-volume, high-velocity backbone of any cross-border ribbon program.

7-Shopee-Lazada-Shopify Workflow, 5-Etsy-eBay-Wayfair-Faire Workflow & 4-Multi-Marketplace PIM

H2-6|Modules 65 through 80 govern the regional and long-tail marketplace layer. The 7 Shopee-Lazada-Shopify modules cover Shopee Mall seller qualification, Lazada LazMall seller qualification, Shopify Plus store build, Shopify app integration (Klaviyo, Yotpo, Loox, Judge.me), Shopify payment gateway, Shopify shipping rate, and Shopify tax engine. The 5 Etsy-eBay-Wayfair-Faire modules cover Etsy handmade-seller qualification (most ribbon SKUs qualify as "handmade-party-supply"), eBay business-seller qualification, Wayfair supplier qualification (typically requires 5+ years of operation, 500+ active SKUs, North-American warehouse), Faire seller qualification (Faire brand vetting, Faire minimum order quantity, Faire payment terms Net-60), and Faire / Wayfair freight routing. The 4 multi-marketplace-PIM modules cover the master SKU record, the marketplace-specific attribute mapping, the marketplace-specific image variant, and the marketplace-specific description variant. The 16 modules in this layer let a brand reach the long-tail marketplaces without re-engineering the SKU master each time.

8-Prohibited-Items-and-Restricted-SKU, 6-Freight-and-3PL-Routing, 5-Customs-and-Duty-Paid & 7-Returns-and-Reverse-Logistics

H2-7|Modules 81 through 106 govern the operational layer. The 8 prohibited-items-and-restricted-SKU modules cover marketplace-specific prohibited items (e.g., Amazon prohibits undeclared magnets, TikTok-Shop restricts some materials, Etsy restricts some categories), restricted SKU (e.g., age-restricted, hazmat-restricted, regulated-restricted), seasonal-restricted SKU, territory-restricted SKU, channel-restricted SKU, IP-restricted SKU (Disney, character, brand), EPR-restricted SKU, and customs-restricted SKU. The 6 freight-and-3PL-routing modules cover Amazon-Partnered-Carrier, Amazon Global Logistics, ocean-FCL / LCL from origin, air-freight from origin, in-territory 3PL warehouse, and last-mile delivery. The 5 customs-and-duty-paid modules cover HTS code, country-of-origin, value declaration, duty-paid by seller (DDP) vs. duty-paid by buyer (DDU), and customs broker. The 7 returns-and-reverse-logistics modules cover FBA return (often the most expensive — typically 15-30% of FBA orders are returned), TikTok-Shop return, Tmall-Global return, Shopee-Lazada return, Etsy-eBay return, Wayfair-Faire return, and refurbish-and-relist. The 26 modules in this layer are what turn a listing into a working cross-border program.

6-Marketplace-Fee-and-Margin, 4-Promotion-and-Seasonal, 7-Customer-Review-and-Feedback & 6-Counterfeit-and-Grey-Market

H2-8|Modules 107 through 128 govern the commercial and protection layer. The 6 marketplace-fee-and-margin modules cover referral fee, FBA fulfillment fee, FBA storage fee, FBA aged-inventory surcharge, advertising cost (PPC), and net margin per SKU per marketplace. The 4 promotion-and-seasonal modules cover Lightning Deal, Prime Day, 11.11 / 6.18 / Black Friday, and seasonal-bundle campaign. The 7 customer-review-and-feedback modules cover review-acquisition (post-delivery email), review-management (response SLA, escalation), review-monitor (sentiment, rating-trend, competitor), review-resolve (refund, replacement, apology), review-amplify (UGC into A+ content), review-defend (counterfeit-report, IP-protection-report), and review-incentive-policy (within Amazon ToS). The 6 counterfeit-and-grey-market modules cover brand-registry enrollment, transparent-program enrollment, project-zero participation, IP-infringement-report cadence, customs-recordal (US CBP, EU customs), and marketplace-takedown SLA. The 23 modules in this layer turn a working program into a profitable and protected program.
