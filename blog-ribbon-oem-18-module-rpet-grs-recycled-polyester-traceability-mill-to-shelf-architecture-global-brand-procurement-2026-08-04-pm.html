Section 1 — The 6-Recycled-Feedstock Source

The 6-source recycled-feedstock architecture is the structural framework for understanding where the rPET chip and fiber come from. The 6 sources are: Source 1 — Post-Consumer PET Bottle (PCR-PET): Clear / blue / mixed-color beverage bottle flake. Most common rPET feedstock. Source 2 — Post-Industrial PET Scrap (PIR-PET): Pre-consumer PET scrap from film, fiber, sheet, strap, bottle. Source 3 — Ocean-Bound Plastic: Coastal PET at risk of entering ocean, certified by OBPC. Source 4 — Marine Plastic (Beach / Sea): Recovered marine PET, certified by OceanCycle. Source 5 — Textile-to-Textile Recycled PET: Chemically or mechanically recycled textile PET, certified by RCS / GRS. Source 6 — Bio-based PET (Partial): Bio-MEG + fossil TPA, partial renewable content. The 6-source map determines the carbon footprint (PCR -32-48% vs virgin), the GRS claim eligibility, and the brand-side marketing claim.

Section 2 — The 8-Chain-of-Custody Stage

The 8-stage chain-of-custody architecture is the structural framework for tracking rPET from feedstock to finished ribbon. The 8 stages are: Stage 1 — Feedstock Collection: Bottle collection center, deposit-return scheme, curbside, ocean clean-up. Stage 2 — Sorting & Flake Production: Optical sorting, wash, flake. Stage 3 — Pelletizing (rPET Chip): Extrusion to rPET chip, IV, color, CO2, yellowing index. Stage 4 — Yarn Spinning: rPET chip to POY / FDY / DTY yarn, denier, filament count. Stage 5 — Weaving / Knitting: Yarn to greige ribbon fabric, width, density, weave structure. Stage 6 — Dyeing / Printing: Color match, dye uptake, print registration. Stage 7 — Finishing / Slitting: Hot stamp, emboss, laser, slitting, edge finish. Stage 8 — Packaging & Shipping: Spool, polybag, carton, pallet, container. The 8-stage chain enables 100% GRS claim and DPP data point.

Section 3 — The 9-GRS-Claim Category

The 9-category GRS-claim architecture is the structural framework for declaring recycled content. The 9 categories are: Category 1 — Pre-Consumer (PIR): Industrial scrap, GRS-eligible. Category 2 — Post-Consumer (PCR): End-consumer PET bottle, GRS-eligible. Category 3 — Mixed Pre-/Post-Consumer: Combined PIR + PCR, GRS-eligible. Category 4 — Recycled Claim Standard (RCS): Lower threshold than GRS, third-party verified. Category 5 — Global Recycled Standard (GRS): Full 4-pillar (recycled content, chain-of-custody, social, environmental, chemical). Category 6 — Ocean-Bound Plastic Claim (OBPC): Specific to ocean-bound feedstock. Category 7 — OceanCycle Marine Plastic: Marine-recovered feedstock. Category 8 — Textile Exchange Preferred Fiber: PCR + bio preferred, MMS (Material Matters Score) tracked. Category 9 — Cradle-to-Cradle Certified (C2C): Full circularity + material health certification. The 9-category stack enables 100% brand claim flexibility per market.

Section 4 — The 7-PCR/PIR Ratio Layer

The 7-rung PCR/PIR ratio architecture is the structural framework for declaring the recycled-content blend. The 7 rungs are: Rung 1 — 100% Virgin PET: Baseline, no recycled. Rung 2 — 25% rPET (20% PCR + 5% PIR): Minimum threshold for ‘recycled’ claim in most markets. Rung 3 — 50% rPET (40% PCR + 10% PIR): Standard GRS claim. Rung 4 — 75% rPET (60% PCR + 15% PIR): High-recycled-content tier. Rung 5 — 100% rPET (80% PCR + 20% PIR): Full GRS-claim. Rung 6 — 100% PCR: Pure post-consumer, premium tier. Rung 7 — 100% Ocean-Bound / Marine: Premium-impact tier. The 7-rung ladder maps to retailer tier (Walmart 50% by 2025, H&M 100% by 2030).

Section 5 — The 5-Recycled-Yarn Specification

The 5-element recycled-yarn architecture is the structural framework for specifying rPET yarn. The 5 elements are: Element 1 — rPET Chip Specification: IV (intrinsic viscosity) 0.62-0.85 dL/g, CO2 (carboxyl end group) ≤35 mmol/kg, color L* / a* / b*, yellowing index ≤8. Element 2 — Yarn Denier & Filament Count: 75D/36F, 75D/72F, 100D/36F, 150D/48F, 300D/96F. Element 3 — Yarn Type: POY, FDY, DTY, ATY, texturized. Element 4 — Tenacity & Elongation: ≥3.8 cN/dtex tenacity, 18-32% elongation. Element 5 — GRS / RCS Certification Number: Per-yarn supplier, scope certificate, transaction certificate per lot. The 5-element spec delivers 100% rPET quality equivalence to virgin.

Section 14 — The 7-End-of-Life Circularity

The 7-strategy end-of-life circularity architecture is the structural framework for keeping rPET in the loop. The 7 strategies are: Strategy 1 — Mechanical Recycling (Closed Loop): Ribbon-to-PET-to-ribbon via shredding, re-extrusion. Strategy 2 — Chemical Recycling (Depolymerization): PET-to-monomer-to-PET, infinite loop. Strategy 3 — Bottle-to-Ribbon (Single Stream): Direct from PET bottle to rPET chip to yarn to ribbon. Strategy 4 — Take-Back Program: Brand-side collection of used ribbon, routed to recycler. Strategy 5 — Industrial Symbiosis: Ribbon scrap to other-industry feedstock. Strategy 6 — Design for Disassembly: Mono-material ribbon, easy to recycle. Strategy 7 — Repair / Reuse (Bow Program): Reuse ribbon bow on multi-occasion gift. The 7-strategy stack delivers 100% circular economy alignment.

Section 15 — The 5-Design-for-Recycle

The 5-rule design-for-recycle architecture is the structural framework for ensuring the ribbon itself is recyclable. The 5 rules are: Rule 1 — Mono-Material Construction: 100% PET, no PVC, no metal, no mixed fiber. Rule 2 — Removable Non-PET Components: Spool, core, label, fastener removable. Rule 3 — Water-Based Ink / Dye: No solvent-based print that contaminates recycling. Rule 4 — No Metallic Foil (or removable): Avoid metallized finish unless it can be separated. Rule 5 — Recyclable Packaging: FSC carton, no plastic overwrap, no mixed material. The 5-rule stack delivers 100% design-for-recycle compliance.
