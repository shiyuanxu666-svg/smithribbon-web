142-Module Architecture Framework: Six Layers, 142 Modules, 100% Brand-Auditable

The 142-module framework organizes the brand-buyer mill-side sustainability-narrative CSR-ESG-disclosure mill-to-shelf decision into six logical layers: (1) Discovery & Frame (modules 1-15), (2) Architecture & Workflow (modules 16-33), (3) Implementation & Pilot (modules 34-56), (4) Operations & Scorecard (modules 57-79), (5) Compliance & Risk (modules 80-96), and (6) Brand-Disclosure & Continuous-Improvement (modules 97-142). Each layer carries between 12 and 25 modules with a defined owner (mill plant manager, mill process engineer, mill ESG-counsel, brand procurement director, brand ESG-CSR director, OEM program-management office), a defined input, a defined output and a defined consumer. The framework is intentionally scalable: a 60-employee single-plant ribbon OEM can run a 142-module lite version, and a 600-employee multi-plant ribbon OEM with China + Vietnam can run the full 142-module enterprise version with a dedicated program-management office and an in-house brand-buyer mill-side sustainability-narrative CSR-ESG-disclosure desk.
